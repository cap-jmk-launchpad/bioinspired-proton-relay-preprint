# A bio-inspired proton-relay mechanism for base-induced polyketide ring closure

Perspective preprint by **Julian M. Kleber** (sail.black, Berlin, ORCID [0000-0001-5518-0932](https://orcid.org/0000-0001-5518-0932)), version 1.0, 27 October 2026.

**PDF:** [`2026-10-27_Kleber_bioinspired-proton-relay-polyketide-ring-closure_v1.0.pdf`](2026-10-27_Kleber_bioinspired-proton-relay-polyketide-ring-closure_v1.0.pdf) (also attached to the [v1.0 release](https://github.com/cap-jmk-launchpad/bioinspired-proton-relay-preprint/releases/tag/v1.0)).

## Summary
Bacteria close the rings of aromatic polyketides such as tetracyclines by aldol condensation inside aromatase/cyclase enzymes. Structures of TcmN and WhiE suggest that one protein-held water molecule moves the protons in this step. We hypothesise that, outside the enzyme and in protic media, these protons travel along a longer hydrogen-bond chain by successive hops (a Grotthuss-type relay), and that the relay can couple successive ring closures. The paper sets out the mechanism, the evidence for and against it, four testable predictions and a computational test plan.

This is a hypothesis paper. It contains no new experimental or computational data.
